# Reference breakdown: "How Ireland Survived Hitler's WWII Invasion Plans"

Source: https://youtu.be/b_57voAnZzw (Crown & Collapse, 7:32). This breakdown is the format target for the workflow, together with the lessons from the first upload.

## What to keep
- The hook formula: a striking contrast, then "Here's why…", then fast stakes.
- One sentence per shot at 4–7 s, rotating maps, figures, scenes and landscapes.
- The relief-map table as the visual anchor for explaining geography and strategy.
- A quotable thesis ending that echoes the hook.

## What the new workflow fixes
1. **The description promised topics the narration never covers.** The description and chapter list name the 1938 Treaty Ports handover, Operation Green, Britain's own plans (Plan W) and the North Strand bombing of Dublin on 31 May 1941. None of these are in the narration. The workflow now builds the description from the final script (Phase 7), and Phase 1 requires the script to cover the named events the topic is known for.
2. **The published chapters were placeholders.** The video went up with "[add your timestamps]" and "[paste link]" still in it, and the chapters had no times. The workflow now measures chapter start times from the timeline and checks for unfilled placeholders.
3. **Some factual lines overstate or are wrong:**
   - "Invading would shatter the myth of German respect for neutral nations": Germany had already invaded neutral Norway, Denmark, the Netherlands and Belgium in 1940.
   - "The Irish army numbered fewer than 10,000": it was small in 1939 but had just over 40,000 men mobilised by early 1941 ([1st Division (Ireland)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Division_(Ireland))).
   - "Ireland produced no coal": Ireland had small coal mines, such as Arigna and Castlecomer. "Little coal and almost no heavy industry" is accurate.

   The workflow now requires a sourced fact sheet and a check of every line against it.
4. **Repetition.** The "no coal, no oil, no steel" argument appears twice, about 4 minutes apart, and many chapter transitions open with "Yet" or "But". The new script rules require one new point per chapter and varied transitions.
5. **Mispronunciations in the auto-captions** ("The Valera", "Barbar Roa", "Laben", "Luftvafer") suggest the TTS or captioning struggled with names. The workflow now includes a one-time pronunciation check for difficult names.

## Suggested corrected chapter plan (if remade)
1. Hook: Europe falls, one island untouched
2. Ireland stays neutral (de Valera, the Emergency)
3. The ports Britain gave back (1938 Treaty Ports, Churchill's anger)
4. Operation Green (Germany's 1940 plan)
5. Britain's secret plan (Plan W)
6. Bombs on Dublin (North Strand, 1941)
7. Why Hitler never came: the sea, the Royal Navy, Barbarossa, little to gain, US opinion
8. Thesis: "…not by strength, but by irrelevance."
