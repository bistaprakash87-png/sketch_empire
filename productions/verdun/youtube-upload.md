## Title
Verdun: The Battle Designed to Bleed France White

## Description (paste as is)
In 1916, Germany's top general planned a battle that wasn't about winning ground. It was built to kill French soldiers faster than France could replace them, and it nearly broke Germany's army too.

For ten months, French and German soldiers fought over a few miles of hills around the fortress city of Verdun. Erich von Falkenhayn's plan, Operation Gericht, relied on more than 1,200 guns and a target France could not afford to lose. This documentary follows how the trap was set, how France held on through the Sacred Way and endless rotations of fresh divisions, and what the battle finally cost both nations: about 714,000 casualties, villages wiped off the map, and a front line that ended almost where it began.

In this video:
• Falkenhayn's "bleed France white" plan, and why historians still argue about it
• 21 February 1916: the opening bombardment, Colonel Driant and the fall of Fort Douaumont
• Pétain, the Voie Sacrée and the noria system that kept France in the fight
• Mort-Homme, Hill 304 and the siege of Fort Vaux, with the pigeon Le Vaillant
• Green Cross gas, Fleury and "They shall not pass"
• The Somme, the Brusilov Offensive and the French counterattacks that retook Douaumont
• The price of Verdun: the lost villages and the Douaumont Ossuary
