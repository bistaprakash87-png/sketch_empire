# Crown & Collapse Style Bible

This style comes from the channel's reference video, *How Ireland Survived Hitler's WWII Invasion Plans*. Every image prompt starts with the **Style prefix**. Then it adds the block for its shot type, the shot's specific content, and any fixed set layout.

## Look in one sentence
A tactile 2.5D paper-cutout diorama: layered cardboard and paper planes with bevelled edges, crisp drop shadows between layers, matte paper textures, a muted historical palette and gentle theatrical lighting, like a museum model photographed up close.

## Style prefix (paste first in every image prompt)
> Layered 2.5D paper-cutout diorama, handcrafted cardboard and matte paper layers with bevelled cut edges and soft drop shadows between layers, subtle paper grain texture, muted historical palette of olive green, slate blue, khaki, charcoal grey and warm brown with selective deep red accents, soft warm key light from upper left, shallow depth of field, clear foreground, middle ground and background layers, 16:9 cinematic composition.

For a 9:16 project, change the last phrase to "9:16 vertical composition".

## Palette
| Use | Colours |
|---|---|
| Base world | olive green, khaki, warm brown, slate blue sea |
| Shadow and metal | charcoal grey, gunmetal |
| Accent: Axis | deep red and black, used sparingly |
| Accent: Allied | navy, muted red-white-blue |
| Ireland and landscapes | moss green, grey-green cliffs, pale gold sunset |
| Paper and documents | aged cream, sepia ink |

Avoid neon, saturated primaries, glossy plastic and photoreal skin.

## Figures
Figures are stylised angular paper-doll and wooden-mannequin people with geometric facial planes, thick dark outlines, simplified hands and period-accurate costume. Faces stay serious and readable with no caricature. Real historical figures keep only their identifying features: hairstyle, moustache, glasses, build and signature clothing.

**Figure block:**
> Stylised angular paper-doll figure with faceted geometric face planes, thick dark outline, simplified hands, period-accurate [uniform/suit] of [year], [defining features], [pose and gaze target].

For cast-sheet references, add: "full body, relaxed three-quarter standing pose, plain neutral grey paper backdrop, even soft light, alone."

Crowds and soldiers use the same style at smaller scale with simplified faces. All people in a shot share one style, so never mix in photoreal or cartoon people.

## Shot-type blocks
**MAP.**
> Carved wooden and cardboard relief map of [region] on a dark wooden table, raised topography, raised border lines, [painted arrows / pins / small ship and tank tokens] showing [movement], small period serif labels reading "[1–3 real words]", top-down three-quarter angle, warm desk-lamp light.

**FIGURE.**
> [Figure block] in [room/setting], [props on table], [lighting mood], medium shot at eye level.

**SCENE.**
> Wide diorama of [event] in [place, year]: [vehicles/troops/ships with counts], [landmark], layered cut-paper scenery receding into haze.

**ARTIFACT.**
> Close-up of [newspaper/telegram/document/flag] on a wooden desk, aged cream paper, bold period headline reading "[2–4 real words]", shallow depth of field, lamp light.

**LANDSCAPE.**
> Atmospheric paper-diorama landscape of [place]: [cliffs/sea/fields/factory], layered depth, [weather/time of day], no people or a tiny lone figure for scale.

## Motion vocabulary
Use a slow push-in, a slow pull-out, a lateral parallax pan across layers, a gentle tilt down onto a map, an arrow drawing itself, pins dropping, tokens sliding along a route, flags rippling, smoke drifting, waves rolling, or a figure making one small gesture: turning a page, pointing, looking out a window.

Keep motion calm and readable, with one camera move and one subject motion per shot. Avoid fast whips, orbits and complex choreography.

## Captions (editor stage)
Word-by-word captions sit at the bottom centre in white bold sans-serif with a black outline, with the spoken word highlighted in yellow, at a maximum of 2 lines.

## Sound
The narration is serious and authoritative at a measured pace. Under it runs a low orchestral or ambient tension bed, with optional quiet effects such as waves, distant artillery or engines when the editor supports them.

## Sensitive imagery
- **Nazi symbols.** Show them only where historically necessary: plain red and black banners and armbands, small in frame. Never make them the focal point of a thumbnail.
- **Violence.** Don't show gore or graphic casualties. Show consequences through rubble, smoke, empty helmets and newspaper headlines.
- **Atrocities.** Treat them with restraint and accuracy, never as spectacle.
